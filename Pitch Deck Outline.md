# Title & High Concept
Title: Drunk Cart Mobile

Tagline: Two-Thumbs, One Cart, Maximum Chaos.

High Concept: A single-player mobile action roguelite where players steer a physics-driven, drifting shopping cart while auto-blasting monster hordes, drinking beer, and trading vehicle handling for overwhelming firepower.

Visual Anchor: Stylized 3D graphic of a turbo-boosted shopping cart drifting around a supermarket aisle corner, spewing magical fireballs into an exploding horde.
# Market Opportunity & Empirical Validation
Market Landscape: The mobile survivor-like genre is a high-grossing category led by Survivor.io (\$500M+ lifetime revenue, \$75M in its first 60 days, \$5M–\$6M monthly pacing) and Archero 2 (\$8.06M in its first 11 days).

The Market Gap: Existing hits rely on static foot movement and walking speeds. Drunk Cart introduces vehicle momentum, drift physics, and physical ramming to solve movement stagnation.

High Monetization Ceiling: Top spending regions demonstrate strong Average Revenue Per Download (\$15.50 RPD in South Korea, \$9.00 in China, \$4.90 in the US), proving high willingness-to-pay for accessible mobile action roguelites.
# Core Mechanical Hook — Power vs. Control
The Intoxication Curve: Defeated enemies drop beer crates. Drinking beer instantly boosts weapon DPS, fire rate, and projectile spread, but continuously decays steering traction.

Continuous Intoxication Stages:
- Sober (0%): Precise joystick steering, baseline damage.
- Tipsy (25%): +15% DPS; minor momentum drift.
- Drunk (50%): Major weapon upgrades unlocked; wheel sliding during sharp turns.
- Very Drunk (75%): Explosive spell synergies; delayed steering responsiveness.
- Wasted (100%): Screen-clearing destruction; heavy camera sway and high-risk drift execution.

Core Player Choice: "Do I tap Drink for immediate DPS to clear this horde, knowing my cart will slide into the next wall?"
# Intuitive Single-Player Touch Controls
Two-Thumb Layout:
- Left Thumb (Virtual Joystick): Steering, acceleration, drift angles, and directional ramming.
- Auto-Shooter System: Primary weapons auto-target nearest/highest-threat targets (matching Vampire Survivors accessibility).
- Right Thumb (1-Tap Action Triggers): Dedicated buttons for Drink Beer, Nitro Boost, and Ram/Brake.
Frictionless Onboarding: Ultra-short tutorial (<30 seconds) designed to get players into the 1-thumb/2-thumb driving-and-shooting loop immediately.
# World Design & 10-Minute Session Arc
Crisp Mobile Run Structure: 10 to 12-minute session arc built for mobile play:
- Minutes 0–3 (Sober Start): Arena orientation, early wave clearance, initial beer pickups.
- Minutes 4–8 (Mid-Run Escalation): Swarm pressure, ranged spitters, destructible aisle hazards, high-speed drifting.
- Minutes 9–12 (Boss Encounter): Facing The Store Manager with aisle lockdowns, hazard waves, and optional Endless Mode.
Environments: Supermarket Aisles, Warehouses, Parking Lots, and Construction Sites.
# Solving Long-Term Retention (Meta-Progression)
The Retention Challenge: Category benchmarks show high Day 1 retention (44%–45%), but sharp decay by Day 7 (15%) if meta-progression lacks depth.
Multi-Axis Progression Architecture:
	In-Run Upgrades: Shooter weapon evolution (Double Shot, Explosive Rounds) + Cart Mechanics (Spiked Bumper, Greased Wheels).
	Out-of-Run Permanent Unlocks: Custom Shopping Cart Chassis (Standard, Dumpster, Turbo Rig), engine tuning, wheel tires, and collectible Beer Brands (Light, IPA, Nitro, Mystery Brew).
# Hybrid Monetization Architecture
Monetization Framework: Hybrid F2P combining battle pass, opt-in rewarded ads, and cosmetic customization:
Cosmetics & Customization: Cart skins, character outfits, particle trails, and horn sound effects.
Seasonal Battle Pass: Thematic content updates (e.g., Black Friday Blowout, Midnight Warehouse Run).
Non-Intrusive Rewarded Ads: Optional 1-up revives, post-run currency multipliers, and daily free shop crates (following the Vampire Survivors / Survivor.io model).
# User Acquisition & Viral UA Strategy
Low CPI Visual Marketing Hook: Absurd visual premise (fantasy character driving a drifting shopping cart through a supermarket) generates high Click-Through Rates (CTR) on short-form feeds.
TikTok / Reels UGC Framework:
	Native-looking organic gameplay clips showcasing high-speed drift crashes and screen-clearing beer explosions.
"Struggling player + gameplay tip" ad formatting to drive community meme sharing and reduce Cost-Per-Install (CPI).
# Production Roadmap & Key Milestones
- Phase 1: Core Mobile Prototype (Weeks 1–4) — Touch steering, drift physics, auto-aim shooter, and 1-tap beer drinking.
- Phase 2: Vertical Slice (Weeks 5–8) — 1 complete Supermarket map, 5 enemy types, 1 Boss fight, functional upgrade tree.
- Phase 3: Soft Launch & Metric Optimization (Weeks 9–12) — Regional test markets to validate 40%+ D1 retention and ad placement engagement.
